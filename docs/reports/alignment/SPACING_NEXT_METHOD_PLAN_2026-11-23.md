## Project direction - 24 November 2026

Prioritize the project’s own mixed-view phone-photo distribution and its box quality over chasing scores on a standardized paper cohort. The best verified model remains YOLO26s with CIoU-NWD on the fixed project validation. Current 16.67/16.72 runs isolate external train-data composition while preserving that evaluation. Next, use 16.70 to test whether crop-level context and deduplication make the external train examples more compatible. ## Decision

Do not merge the IOP gap-box drafts directly into the detector. Their median normalized height was 4.75× the existing-label median and they had only one AI reviewer. The tooth-anatomy transfer experiment has already tested the resulting mask-pretraining hypothesis; see 16.62 above. Preserve the measured result, including its failure to improve AP50-95 materially.

Development target: reach 65% AP50 on the unchanged, reused 477-image validation; 70% is a stretch target. Neither is an expected nor achieved score, and this reused cohort is for paired development comparison, not independent generalization. Do not alter its membership or select cases by model predictions/image quality.

## Additional source and label-protocol screen (23 November 2026)

A targeted search found no new, immediately usable public 2D spacing dataset. Nebiaj et al. (2026) is the closest high-performing protocol reference: 5,854 standardized intraoral photos, with the 15-class analysis reporting 97.9% AP50 for **midline diastema** and 91.0% for **spacing**. The paper defines diastema around the upper central incisors and spacing as a gap with gingiva visible between teeth while excluding midline diastema; diastema labels are predominantly frontal and spacing labels occlusal. Its headline is on a separate held-out set and is not a reproducible score target for this project's merged `gap_or_spacing` label. Use this to audit class/view semantics before any further label merging, and cite the paper as a protocol reference only: https://www.mdpi.com/2304-6767/14/1/60.

Two apparent dataset leads are gated or modality-mismatched and are not added to training: (1) MouthCare's sample has 480 photos/80 patients, tooth/gum/pathology segmentation rather than spacing boxes, and its Hugging Face card explicitly restricts the sample to evaluation and prohibits model training or published research without a separate license: https://huggingface.co/datasets/MouthCare/intraoral-sample. (2) Wang et al.'s orthodontic dataset includes a `Dental spacing` cohort and 3D tooth models, but access requires a Zenodo request and signed DUA; it is not smartphone photography and cannot be scored in the current 2D box benchmark without a separate cross-modal study: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-04138-7. No files were downloaded from these sources.

The 2026 YOLOv11 paper's Data Availability Statement says its 5,854-photo dataset is not public but may be available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request. A request draft is prepared at `PAPER_DATA_ACCESS_REQUEST_DRAFT_NEBIAJ_2026-11-24.md`; it has not been sent. This is the highest-priority exact-label data-access lead. Do not claim access or plan a reproduction until permission, allowed use, and the source split/annotation package are confirmed.

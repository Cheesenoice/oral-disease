Many images use clinical cheek retractors; phone-photo performance remains untested. The full-pool review audit finds zero boxes and zero reviewed candidates. OMNI redistribution terms remain unverified.

The full pool is under `data/processed/alignment_omni_frontal_candidates/`. The original archive SHA-256 and every extracted image SHA-256 were checked against the source queue. An overlap screen found no identical train/validation bytes, but one first-level source folder key occurs in both splits (two train, one validation images). A dHash distance <=4 screen lists 66 candidate cross-split pairs; the first 12 visual pairs include obvious nonmatching mouths, so these are review leads rather than duplicate claims. A conservative exclusion list contains 34 train images pending review. See `docs/reports/alignment/omni_frontal_candidate_audit/`.

Owner-authorized Codex visual research annotations are consolidated in `docs/reports/alignment/agent_visual_pilot/agent_visual_manifest_v4.csv` and `agent_visual_boxes_v4.csv`: 22 original train images and 16 boxes (14 upper-midline gaps, two anterior-crowding candidates). Five low-seam images have gap absent status; crowding is absent in two and uncertain in three. The additional image `201815201808161` is frontal, crowding present and diastema uncertain. These are visual research labels for the school prototype, not clinician-verified ground truth. Notebook 16.12 reproduces v4 from the source batches. No validation rows have yet been reviewed, so the manifest is not ready for training or model selection.

**View rule correction:** the published YOLOv11 malocclusion study reports 5,628 crowding instances on occlusal views, four on buccal views, and none on frontal views. Its crowding box covers the affected tooth and two adjacent teeth. Accordingly, the two frontal crowding boxes in v4 are historical exploratory annotations only and must be excluded from a crowding training export. This branch should train frontal diastema only. Build a separate occlusal crowding dataset if that label remains in scope. See https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12839579/.

[Roboflow Dental Mates](https://universe.roboflow.com/gaiadental/dental-mates) publicly declares CC BY 4.0 and includes a `Spaced` class, but the displayed trained model reports 36.1% mAP50 over its four classes. [DentalMates](https://universe.roboflow.com/gaiadental/dentalmates-zs6b0) declares CC BY 4.0 and lists `misaligned` and `Spaced`, but shows no published dataset version. These broad classes do not establish our upper-midline-gap and anterior-crowding box definitions or the frontal-photo view. They remain audit candidates, not training replacements.

The published [YOLO11 malocclusion study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12839579/) used 5,854 standardized intraoral photographs and reported AP50 97.9% for diastema and 90.1% for crowding on its own dataset; it provides a box-design reference, not an expected result for this project. [SegmentAnyTooth](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11993027/) studies tooth-level localization and segmentation; it does not provide verified labels for our two target findings. Its tooth-localization pipeline is a possible later research direction, not a substitute for target annotations.

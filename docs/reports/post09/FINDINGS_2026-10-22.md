### Fixed operating point

Confidence >=0.25; one-to-one IoU >=0.5. Greedy matching checks unmatched GT first. A nonmatched prediction is an apparent annotation-relative false positive, not a clinically verified healthy negative.

At the same confidence 0.25 on clean images, YOLOv8n has annotation-relative precision/recall 78.71%/95.07%, while YOLO26n has 87.62%/85.96%. The slight AP advantage does not select a deployment operating point. Blur reduces fixed-threshold recall more than its small AP change suggests; scores and thresholds must be considered before selecting a final detector.

There are 611 medium and 443 large annotated boxes and zero small boxes under these definitions. Consequently this validation set provides no estimate of small-box recall. Annotation box area is not necessarily the anatomical extent of a lesion.

Lesion size uses the area after isotropic scaling to maximum edge 640: small <32^2, medium <96^2, otherwise large. Per-size output is recall, not size-specific AP. CPU timings do not establish phone latency. Historical GPU results remain intact; native PR operating points and floating-point/runtime behavior can differ from this CPU re-evaluation.
